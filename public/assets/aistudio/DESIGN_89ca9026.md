## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a fusion of 1990s retro-office tangibility, creative studio photography setups, and precision technical engineering. Designed for developer portfolios and high-craft studio presence, the aesthetic unifies physical desk hardware—frosted vinyl badge sleeves, textured woven straps, physical clip fasteners, archival paper slips—with technical data telemetry and neo-brutalist print layouts.

The design philosophy relies on **Physical Metaphor Architecture**. It bridges skeuomorphic studio realism (cyclorama lighting gradients, tactile hanging ID passes, ambient surface blur, analog film grain) with crisp neo-brutalist discipline (1.5px solid ink borders, hard offset drop shadows, and monospace classification headers).

The emotional tone balances obsessive software craftsmanship with human personality: casual, tilted marker scripts sit comfortably beside tight monospace data tables and monumental geometric headlines.

## Colors

The palette alternates between deep photographic studio emeralds and tactile archival paper neutrals, avoiding flat monochromatic monotony.

### Color Roles & Application
- **Deep Studio Emeralds (`studio-deep`, `studio-emerald-dark`):** Serve as cyclorama backdrop anchors, full-bleed hero stages, and technical HUD terminals.
- **Archival Paper Neutrals (`paper-white`, `paper-tint`, `paper-aged`):** Act as physical paper card faces, editorial project dossiers, and section alternates to maintain high readability.
- **Deep Ink (`ink`, `ink-soft`):** Grounded near-black tone used for crisp 1.5px architectural rules, high-contrast display typography, and spec indices.
- **Mint Accents (`mint-highlight`, `mint-accent`, `studio-light`):** Used for HUD status highlights, active pill indicator tags, and focal text accents within dark surfaces.
- **Material Translucencies (`sleeve-pvc`, `sleeve-border`):** Mimic frosted PVC badges and floating acrylic navigational pills.

## Typography

The typography strategy leverages four deliberate typographic voices:
1. **Monumental Display (`Syne`):** Extra-bold, condensed geometry applied strictly uppercase with tight tracking for section headers and the hero focal identity.
2. **Editorial Interface (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Balanced, modern grotesque handling all body copy, descriptive statements, and primary button labels.
3. **Hardware Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`):** Exacting, industrial monospaced type applied to badge specifications, ledger indices, status pills, and system tags.
4. **Editorial Script Overlay (Hand-drawn marker voice):** Applied casually over geometric headings at `-6deg` angles to break rigid layout planes with an analog studio human touch.

Always prefix classification tags with the technical delimiter `//` (e.g., `01 // DOSSIER`) set in `meta-mono-sm`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model pairs a 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous vertical stage padding (`space-section` / 110px). 

### Layout Model
- **Hero & Staging Area:** Centered spatial depth envelope constrained to viewport units (`clamp(420px, 76svh, 740px)`), allowing hanging lanyard assets and cutout photography to float above content thresholds.
- **Editorial Matrix:** 3-column bento grids (`repeat(3, 1fr)`) with `space-lg` (24px) gutters for technical toolkits, project showcases, and dossier sheets.
- **Asymmetric Split:** 2-column asymmetric distribution (`1.15fr : 0.85fr`) for biographical lead copy paired against the tactile spec sheet card.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Reflow
- **Desktop (> 960px):** Full 12-column grid and 3-column cards active; desk prop accessories and dangling physics elements remain unconstrained.
- **Tablet (≤ 860px):** Asymmetric split collapses to single-column stack. Bento grids and project dossiers drop to a single unified vertical rail.
- **Mobile (≤ 768px):** Navigation links collapse into an elevated pill drawer. Lanyard pass scales down 20% and nests directly inline alongside the profile subject to prevent overlapping critical copy.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is expressed through a synthesis of skeuomorphic physical properties, frosted transparency, and neo-brutalist graphic offsets.

### Depth Stratification
- **Background Layer (z-index 0–1):** Studio cyclorama backdrop gradients combined with an SVG `feTurbulence` analog film grain overlay at 4% opacity (`pointer-events: none`).
- **Surface Layer (z-index 2–3):** Archival paper dossier cards resting flush or raised slightly with neo-brutalist solid drop shadows: `6px 6px 0px var(--studio-mid)`.
- **Physical Prop Layer (z-index 4–5):** Realistic drop shadows for cutouts and lanyard badges:
  - Woven lanyard strap: `0 8px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.22)`
  - Frosted PVC badge sleeve: `0 24px 48px rgba(12, 28, 22, 0.35)` with inner rim highlight `inset 0 1px 2px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`
  - Photographic cutout drop shadow: `filter: drop-shadow(0 22px 36px rgba(10, 25, 20, 0.45))`
- **Frosted Acrylic Overlays (z-index 90):** Top navigation badges with `backdrop-filter: blur(10px)` and soft 1px borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`).

## Shapes

The geometric personality features crisp structured perimeters alongside deliberate capsule/pill elements that simulate manufactured plastics.

- **Dossiers & Cards:** 12px–14px radius with strict 1.5px solid `--ink` outlines.
- **Tactile Badging & Hardware:** Pill capsules (`999px`) for slot punches, floating nav tags, and status chips.
- **Photo Insets & Inner Framing:** Micro-curvatures of 4px to 9px, reflecting die-cut photographic film prints seated inside sleeves.
- **Buttons:** 8px radius for primary solid actions; `999px` full capsule for contextual navigation controls.

## Components

### 1. Hanging Lanyard ID Badge
The primary focal component. Composed of:
- **Woven Neck Strap:** 34px wide with subtle linear herringbone ribbing (`--lanyard-strap`), swinging via pendulum keyframes (`-1.8deg` to `+1.8deg`).
- **Hardware Clip:** Metallic lobster clasp and swivel ring with directional chrome gradients.
- **Frosted PVC Sleeve:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.34)` with 12px backdrop blur, 14px outer radius, 1.5px translucent white border, and an oblong punched slot hole at the top.
- **Inner Printed ID Card:** Crisp white card (`--surface-card`) featuring mono spec tables, passport photograph container (4px radius), barcode stamp, and monumental role title.

### 2. Archival Dossier Cards
- **Structure:** Crisp `--surface-card` or `--paper-white` face bordered with a 1.5px solid `--ink` outline.
- **Offset Shadow:** Fixed hard drop shadow `6px 6px 0px var(--studio-mid)`.
- **Header:** Features a classification label in `meta-mono-sm` preceded by `//`, followed by display project titles and pill metadata tags.
- **Interactive State:** Hover invokes `translateY(-6px)` using cubic-bezier `(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`.

### 3. Terminal HUD Cards
- Applied in technical capability sections.
- Deep emerald background (`--studio-emerald-dark`) framed with translucent mint borders (`rgba(214, 235, 227, 0.15)`).
- Wireframe skeletons, landmark node graphs, and telemetry key-value outputs styled with monospace typography.

### 4. Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Studio Button:** Solid high-contrast white card surface (`#FFFFFF`) with dark `--ink` text, 8px border radius, 1.5px solid `--ink` border, and a 4px 4px 0px `--studio-deep` hard shadow.
- **Frosted Capsule Action:** `rgba(17, 19, 21, 0.35)` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` blur capsule with full pill radius (`999px`) and micro-mono text.

### 5. Chips & Metadata Pills
- Compact inline badges padded `4px 10px` with a 999px radius.
- Backgrounds set to `--mint-highlight` with dark green monospace lettering, or outlined paper-tint capsules with 1px border.

### 6. Form Fields & Inputs
- Archival style: flat `#F5F5F2` background, 1.5px solid `--ink` perimeter, 8px radius.
- Labels rendered in uppercase `meta-mono-sm` with active focus states rendering a solid `3px 3px 0px var(--studio-mid)` offset box shadow.
